SOUL OF THE INDIAN MASTER.

NAKKASI is where the rigorous geometry of global modernism finds its heart in the hands of Indian master craftsmen. We translate the structural language of the avant-garde into the tactile reality of Rosewood and Teak.

Every piece is an act of preservation—of heritage techniques and of silent, powerful forms. From the brutalist edge to the organic curve, we engineering stillness through the medium of the subcontinent’s finest timbers.
