The Nakkasi Narrative:
Global Vision, Local Craft.

Nakkasi is where avant-garde international design meets the unparalleled precision of heritage Indian craftsmanship.

THE PROCESS

Where thought becomes structural.

Our workshop is a dialogue between global aesthetics and local technique. Here, we interrogate materials—indigenous woods and raw metals—until they yield to the demands of modern structural form. We empower master Indian artisans to breathe life into minimalist concepts, ensuring every joint and curve carries the mark of human excellence.

Our Manifesto:
A bridge across borders.

01

Cultural Synergy

We translate foreign design inspiration into the vernacular of Indian excellence. Our pieces are a testament to what happens when global innovation is realized through local mastery.

02

Material Honesty

We celebrate indigenous woods—Sissoo, Teak, and Rosewood—worked entirely by hand. No synthetic masks; only the raw, honest texture of the earth refined by the artisan’s touch.

COBALT SERIES

"Design is the silent storyteller of a culture."

We believe that every piece of furniture carries the narrative of its makers. Our role is to ensure that narrative is one of uncompromising heritage and modern vision.
