COLLECTION 01 — NOIR MONOLITH

THE TEAK MONOLITH

A dialogue between geological permanence and the warmth of organic life. Inspired by the fluid lines of foreign minimalism, the Teak Monolith is hand-carved from solid Indian teak, honoring the living narrative of the wood.

$22,600 SHIPS INTERNATIONALLY

MATERIAL ORIGIN

Sourced from sustainably managed plantations in the Western Ghats of India, each block of teak is seasoned for three years to ensure absolute stability and depth of grain.

CONCEPTUAL ANATOMY

Designed by the NAKKASI Collective, 2024. Hand-carved by master woodworkers in our Saharanpur workshop.

Craft Technique

Traditional Joinery

Utilizing ancient interlocking techniques that require zero metal hardware, allowing the wood to breathe and age naturally.

Finish Logic

Hand-Rubbed Oils

Finished with a proprietary blend of linseed oil and beeswax to highlight the character of the Indian woodcraft heritage.

Dimensions

210 x 95 x 72 CM

Proportioned to anchor an interior with the weight of history and the lightness of modern form.

Provenance

Certified Indian Teak

Each piece is laser-etched with its individual harvest coordinates, tracing its origin back to the forest floor.
